## The three absolutes every lane inherits

Repeated in every L2 prompt because a lane worked in isolation will otherwise breach one.

1. **Nothing outward-facing is ever sent, posted, published, or submitted by an agent.**
   Every external artifact is a file under `docs/growth/drafts/` awaiting the
   maintainer. `E8-L5` makes this mechanically checkable; no lane may weaken it.
2. **No virality claims.** No k-factor, no invite mechanic, no "goes viral" — in copy
   *or* in internal targets. The achievable claim is compounding organic distribution
   off a sharp hook plus published artifacts (`dec-0010` "The honest limit",
   product-marketing §7). A target that assumes virality manufactures a failure.
3. **Every channel carries a pre-registered numeric threshold** — a rate with an
   n-minimum and a deadline, set before the channel runs. Post-hoc "that counts"
   reasoning is the failure mode `/growth-experiments` exists to prevent.

## The dependency nobody may paper over

**There is no `dira` binary.** `go build` has nothing to build; `dira check` does not
exist; `brew install kazi-org/tap/dira` fails. E0–E3 must ship before anything can be
recorded, installed, or launched. Consequences, stated once:

- `E8-L4` (record the clips) **cannot start today.** Its `depends_on` says so.
- `E8-L6` (launch) is gated the same way, and its readiness checker must **exit
  non-zero today, naming the missing binary** rather than reporting a green plan.
- `E8-L1`, `E8-L2`, `E8-L3`, `E8-L5` are genuinely workable now — they are copy,
  fixtures, static HTML, packaging, and checkers.
- Every dated thing in this epic is relative to **T0 = the day the binary is
  installable by a stranger.** No absolute launch date appears anywhere in E8.

## Instruments

`/distribution` supplies the channel framework (Bullseye 19-channel selection with
introvert/engineer fit ratings, the HN/Reddit playbooks and their etiquette tripwires,
engineering-as-marketing, launch sequencing). `/growth-experiments` supplies the
threshold discipline (ICE backlog, pre-registered spec shape, terminal metric,
one-iteration rule). **Neither framework is to be reinvented** — no lane invents its
own channel taxonomy or its own scoring function.

Reusable precedent already exists in the sibling repo and is cheaper to copy than to
rediscover: `kazi/site/scripts/check-coherence.mjs` (canonical strings must appear
verbatim in the README or CI fails), `kazi/priv/examples/hero_cast_demo/record.sh`
(the committed cast is produced only by a committed script), and
`kazi/docs/marketing/launch-plan.md` (phase sequencing with a gate before any send).

### E8-L1 — Select the channels and pre-register a numeric threshold for each

**Deliverable:** `docs/growth/channels.md` (the Bullseye pass), `docs/growth/experiments.md`
(the ICE backlog + one pre-registered spec per inner-ring channel), `docs/growth/corpus.md`
(seeded, schema only), `docs/growth/scripts/check-growth-plan.mjs`.

**acc:** `node docs/growth/scripts/check-growth-plan.mjs` exits 0 against the committed
plan, and exits non-zero with a named reason against each of three committed bad
fixtures — one whose inner-ring channel row omits owner or cadence, one whose threshold
states a raw count with no denominator or no n-minimum, and one containing a banned-hype
term outside the marked honest-limits block; the checker itself asserts 19 channels
rated and exactly 3 in the inner ring.

**risk:** Thresholds get set to whatever the channel is likely to produce rather than to
what would actually justify continuing, so every experiment passes and the plan learns
nothing. A threshold David would not act on failing is decoration, not a predicate.

**entries:** implements `dec-0010` (the loop and its honest limit); constrained by
`cst-0004` (the renderer is additive — no channel may depend on a hosted tier existing),
`dec-0007` (never charge for the CLI, hooks, skill, `ui`, or renderer), `int-0003`.

### E8-L2 — Build the landing page on the existing design system and gate it

**Deliverable:** `docs/design/landing/index.html` on `docs/design/tokens.css`; `render.mjs`
extended to enumerate `landing/`; `docs/design/scripts/contrast.mjs` — **which does not
exist in the repo today** although `DESIGN.md` cites it as a standing test; canonical
strings + a coherence gate against `README.md` and `.agents/product-marketing.md`.

**acc:** `node docs/design/scripts/render.mjs r5 landing` captures 6 shots (3 viewports ×
2 schemes) and prints `GATE PASS`; `node docs/design/scripts/contrast.mjs` reports 0
failures with hover exceeding rest on all six surface/scheme combinations and exits
non-zero when a colour token is edited to a failing value; `grep -oE '#[0-9a-fA-F]{3,6}'
docs/design/landing/index.html` returns only `#0f151c` and `#f7f4ed`; and
`node docs/design/scripts/check-coherence.mjs` fails when the hook, the tagline, the
install line, or the no-binary status line diverges from `README.md`.

**risk:** The landing page becomes a third positioning surface that drifts from the README
and the marketing doc within a week. The coherence gate is the only thing preventing it,
so it must cover the hook, tagline, install line, and status line — not just the title.
Second-order risk: a page showing a working `brew install` before E0 ships is a
vaporware claim to the one audience that checks.

**entries:** implements `dec-0010` (the attribution line stays one quiet sentence, never a
badge; the acquisition surface must decode `int-`/`dec-`/`qst-` for a stranger);
constrained by `cst-0004` (state explicitly that the renderer is additive and the CLI is
identical without it), `dec-0007`, `int-0001` (copy source: 83 ADRs, unread).

### E8-L3 — Build the demo fixture ledger and the deterministic recording harness

**Deliverable:** `fixtures/demo-ledger/` (a real ledger containing `dec-0060` with the
rejected "a daemon" alternative, its `why_not`, its `revisit_if`, and the `int-0002` it
cites), `assets/demo/record-check.sh`, `assets/demo/record-init.sh`, `assets/demo/README.md`,
and a checker binding the harness to product-marketing §6.

**acc:** a zero-dependency node checker validates every file in `fixtures/demo-ledger/`
against the `required` fields and closed `kind` enum of `schema/entry.schema.json`,
reports 0 errors, and fails on a committed fixture with an out-of-set kind; `dec-0060`
has a non-empty `alternatives[].why_not`; `assets/demo/record-check.sh` exits non-zero
with a named error when `dira` is absent from PATH and never emits a cast file; and
`node docs/growth/scripts/check-demo-script.mjs` asserts the typed command and every
expected output line in the harness appear **verbatim** in `.agents/product-marketing.md`
§6, failing on a single changed character.

**risk:** The fixture gets tuned to make the clip look good, so the clip demonstrates
behaviour `dira check` does not have. Mitigation is not discipline but sharing: this must
be the **same** fixture E3's `acc` line consumes ("a fixture ledger containing dec-0060"),
not a private prop for marketing. If E3's lane authors its own, one of the two is lying.

**entries:** constrained by `dec-0002` (one file per entry), `dec-0003` (a regex-tier
entry is `staged`, never accepted — a demo that shows auto-accepted entries misrepresents
the product), `cst-0002` (five kinds, closed), `cst-0001`.

### E8-L4 — Record and cut the two demo clips — **BLOCKED: no binary**

**Deliverable:** `assets/demo/check.cast` + its social cut (primary, ≤20s, the `dira check`
catch) and `assets/demo/init.cast` + cut (secondary, ≤60s, `dira init` on a real repo
producing a graph and the contradictions already in it).

**acc:** a committed duration probe reports `assets/demo/check.cast` ≤20.0s from its final
frame timestamp and `assets/demo/init.cast` ≤60.0s; `ffprobe -show_streams` on both
rendered cuts reports **zero audio streams** (no narration); every line in each cast body
appears verbatim in the L3 harness output, so a hand-recorded cast that drifts from the
script fails; and the `init` cast's rendered ledger is non-empty — an empty first run is a
`dec-0010` violation, not a clip to ship.

**risk:** Re-recorded by hand under time pressure, the cast diverges from the committed
harness and stops being reproducible — at which point nobody can regenerate it after a
CLI output change, and the highest-value asset silently rots. The verbatim check is what
makes that fail loudly.

**depends_on, honestly:** `E8-L3` for the harness and fixture, and **E0 → E1 → E2 → E3**
for a binary that can run `dira check` and `dira init` at all. This lane has no start date
and must not be given one; it starts when `dira check` exits non-zero against
`fixtures/demo-ledger/` on a real machine.

**entries:** `dec-0010` (no empty first run), `dec-0003`, `int-0001`.

### E8-L5 — Package the Claude Code ecosystem placement as approval-gated drafts

Channel 1 by leverage: the ICP is definitionally already inside the Claude Code
ecosystem, and kazi's marketplace rails exist (`kazi-org/claude-plugins`, published from
kazi's release pipeline so plugin content can never lag the binary).

**Deliverable:** `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` and its bundle layout, plus
`docs/growth/drafts/{marketplace-listing,awesome-list-prs,directory-submissions}.md`, plus
`docs/growth/scripts/check-drafts.mjs` — the mechanical form of absolute #1.

**acc:** `node docs/growth/scripts/check-drafts.mjs` exits 0 only when every file under
`docs/growth/drafts/` carries frontmatter `status: awaiting-maintainer-approval` and
`posted: false`, and exits non-zero when a fixture draft sets `posted: true` or when any
file under `docs/growth/` or `assets/demo/` contains an automated-post invocation from a
committed deny-list (HTTP POST to a social/forum API host, `praw`, `tweepy`,
`gh pr create`, `gh issue create`); and `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` parses, with its
declared hook commands matching `hooks/` name-for-name and the check failing when a hook
is renamed on one side only.

**risk:** Two collisions. (a) Ownership: E2 owns the skill's *content* and
`dira install-hooks`; this lane owns only *packaging and listing*. If E2's lane also
writes `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`, one of them silently wins. (b) Versioning: if the
marketplace bundle ships independently of the binary, plugin content lags the binary —
kazi already solved this by publishing the bundle from the release pipeline, and copying
that is cheaper than rediscovering the bug.

**entries:** constrained by `dec-0008` (merge-never-clobber settings contract; integration
only via kazi's public `--json` surface), `cst-0004`, `dec-0007` (the plugin is free,
forever).

### E8-L6 — Sequence the launch and open the GTM section of the roadmap

**Deliverable:** `docs/growth/launch.md` (phases relative to T0, per `/distribution`'s
launch playbook), the outward-facing drafts (`show-hn.md` with title + first comment,
the Reddit post plus its 9:1 contribution ledger, the X/Bluesky thread), the GTM section
added to `docs/roadmap.md`, and `docs/growth/scripts/check-launch-readiness.mjs`.

**acc:** `node docs/growth/scripts/check-launch-readiness.mjs` **exits non-zero today,
naming the absent binary as the blocking gate**, and exits 0 only when all of these are
objectively true: every step in `launch.md` names an owner and an offset from T0 with no
absolute date anywhere in the file; every channel named in `launch.md` id-matches a
pre-registered threshold row in `docs/growth/experiments.md` with no orphan on either
side; `check-drafts.mjs` passes; the ≤20s cast probe from E8-L4 passes; `docs/roadmap.md`
contains a GTM section whose every item names an owner and a PR number; and the HN title
draft matches `^Show HN: dira – ` and contains no term from the banned-hype lexicon.

**risk:** `launch.md` acquires an implied date and starts being read as a commitment,
which then pressures E0–E3 into shipping to a marketing calendar. T0 is defined as "the
day a stranger can install it", never a date. Secondary risk: the roadmap GTM section
duplicates status that `docs/roadmap.md` already owns for E0–E7 — it lists GTM items
only, and links rather than restates.

**entries:** `dec-0010`, `cst-0004`, `dec-0007`; and `qst-0003` — **read this before
sequencing.** `dec-0010` promoted ADR-import quality to a launch blocker, so the
secondary clip and the day-1 value claim both rest on an open question. If import yields
entries with empty `alternatives`, the `init` clip has nothing to show and the launch
sequence is built on a promise the product does not keep.
