   - two bucket vocabularies, and **no top-level `running` or `done` array** in the JSON;
   - `by_repo` is **not deduped and carries no timestamps** — 63 entries over 35 distinct
     `goal_ref`s on a real machine, 20 refs with more than one run, `warnings-clean` with
     4;
   - `by_repo.in_progress` catches `terminated` runs, so it does not mean "live";
   - therefore per-goal truth comes from `kazi status <ref> --json`, which **is** a

     `proposed` and `approved` and is never filtered, so the bridge is durable — but a
     **rejected** proposal disappears from portfolio entirely;

     `totals.rows`;
   - `portfolio` is machine-global, so filter to the `goal_ref`s your own `realized_by`
     edges name.

## The specific thing not to do

Do not resolve a multi-run goal by taking the first `by_repo` entry. It passes any
single-run fixture and is wrong on 20 of 35 real refs, in the direction that matters —
a converged run and a stuck retry of the same goal sit side by side with nothing to
order them. Either call `Status(ref)` or report `Ambiguous`.
