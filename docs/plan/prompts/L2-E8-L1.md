- **A threshold with no denominator is not a threshold.** "≥50 upvotes" is a vanity count;

  predicate. The checker enforces the shape, so define the shape before you write it.
- **Gate zero applies.** `/growth-experiments` puts a PMF check before acquisition spend,
  and dira has no users, no retention curve, and no binary. Say in `channels.md` which of
  the inner-circle channels are pre-PMF *learning* experiments versus post-PMF
  acquisition spend, and do not schedule paid acquisition against an unretained product.
