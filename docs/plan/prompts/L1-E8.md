collectively satisfy it; no single lane needs to.

## Positioning is already done. Do not re-derive it.

`.agents/product-marketing.md` is the positioning context every marketing skill reads
first. It settles the category (*decision memory for AI coding agents*), the ICP, the
messaging hierarchy, the tagline ("Never explain the same decision twice"), the demo
asset, channel priority, and the objections. **Read it and build on it.** If you think
something in it is wrong, say so explicitly rather than quietly substituting your own.

## Scope

The distribution plan, the landing page, the `dira check` demo clip, and the launch
sequence. `docs/roadmap.md` has no GTM section — add one.

## Specific things to get right

- **The `/distribution` skill is installed** and is the right instrument: Bullseye
  19-channel selection with introvert/engineer fit ratings, HN/Reddit playbooks with
  etiquette guardrails, engineering-as-marketing, launch sequencing. Use it rather
  than inventing a channel framework. `/growth-experiments` is the right instrument
  for the numeric thresholds.
- **Every channel needs a pre-registered numeric threshold**, set before launch, or
  the plan cannot tell success from noise.
- **Do not promise virality.** A single-player developer tool has no invite mechanic
  and no k-factor. What is achievable is compounding organic distribution off a sharp
  hook plus published artifacts inside a fast-growing ecosystem. The positioning doc
  says this; internal targets must too, or a missed bar will read as failure.
- **Nothing outward-facing is sent, posted, or published by an agent.** Everything is
  a draft for the maintainer's approval. This is absolute.
- **The launch has a hard prerequisite: there is no binary.** E0–E2 must ship first.
  Plan the assets now — they sharpen what the product must do — but the launch lane's
  `depends_on` must state the dependency honestly rather than assuming a date.
- The demo clip is the single highest-value asset: `dira check` catching a
  relitigation, ≤20s, no narration needed. The secondary clip is `dira init` on a real
  repo producing a graph plus contradictions in 60s.
