## The unresolved thing you must resolve — and record

**"≤1500 tokens measured by the binary" has no tokenizer behind it yet.** dira cannot
call a model (`dec-0003`), must not fetch a BPE file at runtime (`cst-0004`,
`int-0002`), and must stay under a ~100ms cold budget (`E1-L6`). So the counter is
either a vendored tokenizer or a documented offline estimator, and if it under-counts,
the constitutional cap is decorative.

Plan a task that **chooses and records the choice as a new `.dira/` decision entry**
with its alternatives and `why_not`s (vendored BPE tables versus a conservative
character/word estimator versus a byte budget). Two properties are non-negotiable: the
counter is deterministic and offline, and its error direction is **conservative** —
when uncertain it drops more rather than less. Whatever you choose, the entry must say
what "token" means in `brief.max_tokens`, because a user reading 1,500 will assume a
model's tokenizer.

This is a decision inside the lane, not a blocker on the lane. Do not stop for it —
but do not let it get made implicitly by whichever function ships first, either.

## Constraints that bind this lane

- **Drop, never truncate.** Over-cap means whole entries removed from the
  low-priority end with a stated omission. A mid-render cut is a defect, and it is the
  specific failure `cst-0001` names.
- **Two forms, one selection.** `dira brief` is human-facing; `dira brief --context`
  is the agent-facing form injected into the session. Both obey the cap.
- **`--chain` is a defined degradation in E1**, not a stub — see *Pinned
  interpretations* 3. Tier resolution is E5 and is BLOCKED on `qst-0001`; do not plan
  parent-ledger traversal.
- **Fail open.** The hook wraps the command in `|| true` with a 5s timeout, but a
  brief that exits non-zero on a malformed entry file silently gives the session no
  orientation at all. A single unparseable entry must degrade to a brief without it,
  saying so — not to an empty brief.
- **No execution status** (`dec-0004`). "Open blockers" in E1 means open questions
  carrying a `blocks` edge, not kazi's blocked bucket. E4 adds the join; if you render
  a slot for it now, it must be absent rather than guessed.
- **No network, no daemon** (`int-0002`, `cst-0004`).
