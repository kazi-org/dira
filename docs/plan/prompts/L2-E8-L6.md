## T0 is a condition, never a date

**T0 = the day a stranger can install dira and have it work.** Every step in `launch.md`
is an offset from T0 (`T0−14`, `T0`, `T0+7`), and the checker fails on any absolute date in
the file. This is not pedantry: a launch document with a date on it starts being read as a
commitment, and then pressures E0–E3 into shipping to a marketing calendar. That failure
mode is the one thing this lane can do most damage with.

## The absolutes

1. **Nothing outward-facing is ever sent, posted, published, or submitted by an agent.**
   Every post is a draft under `docs/growth/drafts/`, validating under E8-L5's
   `check-drafts.mjs`, awaiting the maintainer. No task may post, schedule a post, DM, or
   email anyone. This is absolute and no launch urgency overrides it.
2. **No virality claims** in the launch plan or its internal targets. Compounding organic
   distribution off a sharp hook plus published artifacts is the claim. A target that
   assumes virality manufactures a failure that will be read as the product's.
3. **Every channel in `launch.md` id-matches a pre-registered threshold** in
   `docs/growth/experiments.md`. Orphans in either direction fail the checker: a channel
   with no threshold cannot tell success from noise, and a threshold with no channel is a
   plan that was quietly abandoned.
4. **No launch without a binary.** The readiness checker's first gate is that
   `brew install kazi-org/tap/dira` (or the direct download E0 produces) actually installs
   a working binary. Until then the checker exits non-zero, by design.

will be ignored; if it prints the ordered list of gates with the first unmet one named, it
becomes the launch checklist itself.
