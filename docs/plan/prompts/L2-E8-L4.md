## Absolutes

1. **Nothing outward-facing is published by an agent.** The clips are committed assets and
   drafts. Posting, uploading, or embedding them anywhere external is the maintainer's
   action, and no task may do it.
2. **The cast is produced only by the committed script.** A hand-recorded or hand-edited
   cast that no longer matches the harness is a rotting asset — nobody can regenerate it
   after a CLI output change. The verbatim check is what makes that fail loudly, so it
   must be part of the acceptance, not a nice-to-have.
3. **No fabricated output.** If a frame shows something the binary did not print, the clip
   is a lie about working software. Do not plan a "mock the output for now" task.
4. **No narration and no music** — the zero-audio-stream check is the mechanical form of
   "needs no narration". Captions burned in are also out: if the frame needs explaining,
   the wrong frame was chosen.
