`dira brief` is injected into every session and read by a human at a glance. Both
consumers have hard limits: context is finite and attention is shorter.

The ceiling is enforced by the binary (`brief.max_tokens`), not by editorial
judgment. When the brief would exceed it, dira **drops entries by priority rather
than truncating mid-render** — open blockers first, then current focus, then recent
decisions — and states what it omitted plus the verb to see the rest.

This is constitutional because the failure it prevents is dira's specific death:
a brief that grows without bound becomes the 77-ADR pile with better syntax, and
nobody reads it either. Raising the number requires superseding this constraint,
in writing, with a reason.
