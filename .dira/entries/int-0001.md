Hundreds of hours of brainstorming produce agreement that then evaporates. The
agreement is not lost because it was never written down — it was written down, as
77+ ADRs. It is lost because nobody re-reads files, and iteration outruns reading.

So the target is not better capture. It is **retrieval that costs zero effort**:
the rationale arrives unbidden, at the moment it is relevant, in the context of
whoever needs it — human or agent. If reading dira is ever a task on someone's
list, dira has failed and is just a nicer ADR pile.

Achieved when: a decision made in week 1 correctly shapes a session in week 12
without anyone opening a file, and an attempt to relitigate it is met with its own
recorded reasoning before a single line of code is planned.
