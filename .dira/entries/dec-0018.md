A `⊙` mark and a `withheld` status in `--bearing`, sitting in the same column position
where other rows read `accepted 30 Jul` or `informs`.

**Only orphan is drift, and only orphan may use `--caught`** (Law 1). Withheld reads as
neither an error nor a warning — that was the whole difficulty, and it is why the three
states were rendered *adjacent*, with the real red drift card in the rail, so withheld
was judged against the alarm it must not resemble rather than in isolation.

## The cost, recorded rather than absorbed

`--bearing` now carries **two** documented meanings — the instrument, and withheld. The
hue budget says one meaning per hue and a hue with no role fails review. This is a
deliberate exception, taken because withheld *is* an instrument state: it is the compass
reporting what it can and cannot resolve. A fourth hue would have been the alternative
and would have cost more.

## What the founder caught

The first cut said the same thing three times — a prose clause, a status word, and the
Arising sentence. The row is now `sire:int-0002  private ledger   ⊙ withheld`, and the
plain-language explanation appears once, in the Arising line, where a stranger reads.
That also fixed a hierarchy inversion nobody had named: withheld was rendering as the
visually heaviest row in the chain, and withheld is the unremarkable state.
