Obsidian, Linear, and a drawer of half-adopted tools each failed at the same layer
and were kept for the same reason: none of them owned direction, so none of them
could be deleted. dira is only worth building if adopting it lets tools leave.

The discipline this imposes is subtractive, and it cuts against every instinct to
add features: dira takes the direction layer and **nothing else**. Not tasks, not
calendar, not journal, not notes-as-a-second-brain. Execution belongs to kazi;
doneness belongs to kazi; scheduling belongs elsewhere. The closed entry set
(cst-0002) is the structural guard on this intent — every proposed sixth entry
kind is a proposal to become Obsidian.

Achieved when: at least two tools are uninstalled and not missed, and no session
needs a surface outside Claude Code, kazi, and dira to answer "what are we doing
and why."

**Sharpened 2026-07-30 (`dec-0013`).** "At least two tools" named no tool and no date,
so nothing could falsify this intent. The first specific target is now
`~/.claude/skills/sitrep` — a hand-executed version of `dira map` whose gather phase
reads five sources and hand-glosses every id. It reduces to a judgement layer over
`dira map` plus `gh`, or the tool-reduction thesis is weaker than claimed. Note the
target is *reduction*, not deletion: the verdict, the weighted percentage, the risks
and the trend are judgement and stay with the human.
